Assumptions and reproducibility

The spatial variation is computed directly at site coordinates. PLZ serve only for display. Separate, date-bound random streams drive the spatial field and the parcel draws. Extended runs do not change earlier days. Daily draws of both variants use the same random numbers.

Comparison data and configuration · Source and code hashes

The comparison isolates spatial redistribution. Calendar profiles, real regional volume fluctuations and future development must subsequently be calibrated or justified separately.
